03

Scoring system

Public federal data at census-tract resolution

What goes into the score
  • HailNRI hail risk: how often damaging hail falls times the value exposed, scored 0 to 100 nationally. Half of Texas housing scores above 88.
  • TornadoNRI tornado risk: shown in the tables and the spreadsheet.
  • CoastalNRI coastal flood and hurricane risk: used to flag the coastal ZIPs in section 05.
  • TiersBecause almost all of Texas is above the national median for hail, the tiers are set within Texas: Extreme hail score 97+, High 93 to 97, Moderate 80 to 93, Low under 80.
Reference points
  • North TexasAllen 75002: hail 98 (Extreme); Fort Worth 76102: hail 99 (Extreme); Dallas 75201: hail 96 (High)
  • Your leads' coreHouston 77083: hail 31 (Low); Baytown 77521: hail 33 (Low); Houston 77095: hail 26 (Low)
  • Your home marketWebster 77598: hail 23 (Low); League City 77573: hail 42 (Low); Friendswood 77546: hail 40 (Low)

Houston and the coast

Your home market · Harris · Galveston · Fort Bend · Montgomery · Brazoria and the Gulf Coast

Coastal ZIPInland ZIP
Coastal ZIPs have a coastal flood score of 50+ or at least 20% of residents in tracts rated high for coastal flooding. Webster 77598 is inland by this measure; League City, Friendswood, Seabrook, Kemah and Houston 77058, 77059 and 77062 around it are coastal.
What the numbers say
  • HouseholdsHouston Metro and the Gulf Coast hold 3,063,741 households, 28% of the state.
  • Your leads2,025 of 4,499 leads (45%) came from this area: 1,942 Home and 83 Auto. Houston Metro alone supplied 1,818, against 24% of Texas households.
  • Around your officeWebster, League City, Friendswood, Seabrook, Kemah and Houston 77058, 77059 and 77062 produced 84 leads together.
  • Your officeWebster 77598 scores 23 for hail (Low), with a median household income of $60,977; it scores 90 for hurricane wind.
  • Wind and waterEvery Houston Metro ZIP but one is Low for hail; the coastal exposure there is hurricane and flood, so wind deductibles and flood coverage come up on most Home quotes.

Hail in the conversation

Roofs on Home, comprehensive on Auto

Why it matters here
  • CoverageHail damage to a car is paid under comprehensive coverage. A shopper switching on liability only has no hail protection; ask about it on every quote.
  • DeductiblesIn High and Extreme ZIPs the comprehensive deductible is worth explaining at quote time, before the first spring storm.
  • RoofsRoof age and material drive Home price and wind and hail deductibles in Texas; ask on every Home quote.
